Day 0 — Roadmap delivery

Your Digital Marketing career roadmap is ready

Here is the information you requested about our digital marketing pathway and cohort schedules.

Thank you for reaching out to explore our Digital Marketing programme. We understand that choosing the right path for your career transition is a significant decision, and we are here to provide the clarity you need.

Attached, you will find the details regarding our practical, industry-aligned curriculum and the current intake schedule. Our approach focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world execution, ensuring you develop the technical skills highly relevant in today’s digital economy.

When you have had a moment to review the documentation, please reply to this email if you have any questions or would like to schedule a brief consultation to discuss your specific career goals and eligibility. We are happy to assist you in mapping out your next steps.

Day 3 — Proof / case study

Building a career with our hiring network

See how we connect our learners to a network of over 1,000 active hiring partners.

In our consultation sessions, the most common question we receive relates to job market readiness. It is natural to feel cautious, which is why we emphasize practical experience alongside an established hiring infrastructure.

Since our founding in 1987, we have cultivated a network of over 1,000 hiring partners who actively engage with our graduates. This ecosystem is designed to help our learners bridge the transition from student to professional by aligning our training with the specific technical requirements employers currently prioritize.

If you are curious to learn more about how this network supports your transition or want to see examples of the types of roles our learners typically pursue, please reply to this email. I would be glad to share more context on how we facilitate these connections.

Regards,
Admissions Team

Day 7 — Final prompt

Ready to discuss your career pathway?

This is a quick check-in to see if you have any remaining questions about our programme intake.

I wanted to reach out one last time to see if you had any follow-up questions regarding the Digital Marketing programme or the enrolment process. Our admissions team is currently finalising the intake details for our next cohort, and we want to ensure you have all the information required to make an informed choice.

Choosing a learning partner is a commitment of time and effort, and we aim to be as transparent as possible about what to expect throughout the programme. Whether you are still deciding or simply require more clarity on the curriculum, we are available to help.

If you would like to move forward with a career-pathway consultation, simply reply to this email with your preferred availability. We look forward to supporting you in your professional journey.

Regards,
Admissions Team
